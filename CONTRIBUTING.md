# Contributing to PyLCSS

PyLCSS welcomes issue reports and proposed changes, but it is source-available,
not Open Source Initiative-approved open-source software. The project itself is
licensed under PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 plus the additional LGPL compatibility
permission in `LICENSE`.

By submitting a contribution for inclusion, you represent that you have the
right to submit it and agree that the contribution may be distributed under
the current PyLCSS project license. Clearly identify third-party code, data,
fonts, models, or generated material and include its source, copyright notice,
license, and any modification notice. Do not submit material whose terms are
incompatible with the project or whose provenance is unclear.

For substantial contributions, open an issue first so licensing, scope, and
technical direction can be agreed before significant work is undertaken.
